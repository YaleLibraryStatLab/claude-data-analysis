# Data

Workshop datasets and their codebooks. Everything in this folder is a
read-only input: analyses read from here and write under `scripts/`.

| Folder | Used in | Contents |
|---|---|---|
| `kam-palmer/` | I Do | 1,254 respondents from the 1965--1973 Political Socialization Panel Study |
| `salaries/` | We Do | Nine-month salaries for 397 faculty at one US college, 2008--09 |
| `airquality/` | They Do | 153 daily air-quality observations, New York, May--September 1973 |
