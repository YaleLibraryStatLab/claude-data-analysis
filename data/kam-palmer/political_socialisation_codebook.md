# Codebook: 1965–1973 Political Socialization Panel Study

**106 variables. Outcome measured 1973; all covariates measured 1965.**

---

## Identifiers

| Variable | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| interviewid | Unique respondent identifier |
| college | Whether respondent attended college (treatment variable) |

## Outcome

| Variable | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| yppnscal | Youth political participation scale: additive index of eight acts reported in 1973 (voted in 1972, attended rallies, displayed button/sticker, worked on campaign, donated, contacted official, demonstrated, worked on local issue) |
